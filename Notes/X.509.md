The standard for a certificate: a public key bound to an identity by a third
party's signature, and the foundation of every [[Public Key Infrastructure]].

It was standardised in 1988 inside X.500, a telco project to build a global
telephone book, which is why a certificate still carries a locality, a state
and a country.
