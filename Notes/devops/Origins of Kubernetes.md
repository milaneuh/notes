Originally, Kubernetes was an internal tool in Google called `Borg` to manage their clusters of micro-services. In 2013 this project was open-sourced with the help of the [[linux]] foundation.

> Before k8s, there were other ways to manage cluster such as Docker Swarm or Mesos
